Culinary Guide to Cengkareng & Surrounding Areas

Cengkareng and its neighboring spots (Taman Palem, Citra Garden, Daan Mogot, Kamal/Menceng) offer a wide variety of dining options—ranging from established regional specialties to local hidden gems.

Frequently Asked Questions & Comprehensive Guide

Question 1: What are the hidden gems, standout favorites, and regional ethnic specialties in Cengkareng?

Cengkareng and its neighboring spots (Taman Palem, Citra Garden, Daan Mogot, Kamal/Menceng) offer a wide variety of dining options—ranging from established regional specialties to local hidden gems. Standout hidden gems include: RM Thua To (famous for its signature Lo Mie); RM Segar Wangi in Citra 8 (known for fresh seafood near the fish market, featuring Sup Kepala Salmon as a standout recommendation and fan-favorite Cumi Goreng Tepung); RM Pinang Sari in Ruko Citra 2 shophouses (notable for the owner's child selling stationery in front of the cashier); Bakmi Air Mangkok near Superindo Taman Palem (serving authentic Bangka-style noodle dishes); Mie Sop Medan next to the Damkar Palem Fire Station (serving authentic Mie Sop Medan paired with Sate Kerang clam skewers); and RM Ci Hera / Ci Hela (highly rated home-style cooking). Regional and ethnic specialties feature noodle spots like Bakmi A Khiong, Bakmi Kemanggisan, Bakmi Shanghai, and Bakmi Siantar; Kwetiau dishes like Kwetiau Siram Akung (famous for family celebrations) and Kwetiau Goreng A Cien; Medan & Bagan Siapi-Api traditional delicacies from North Sumatra and Riau concentrated in Pasar Laris Taman Surya; and Ahua Chinese Food near the Citra 6 horse statue.

Question 2: Where are the key dining hubs, street food corridors, malls, and local quick bite spots in Cengkareng?

Key area hubs include the Citra 8 & Lake Area (a major recreation hub with a scenic boardwalk lined with restaurants and cafes); the Taman Palem Hub main boulevard (a competitive strip featuring Warung Rawit and Warung Kita); Jalan Kayu Besar (famous for late-night comfort food like Bebek Goreng Madura and Nasi Goreng Gila); and Jalan Menceng Raya along the Kamal Raya corridor (lined with food stalls like Martabak Taraso and numerous RM Padang). For Daan Mogot and mall areas, key spots include Bebek Kaleyo in Daan Mogot, Sate Senayan near the Citra 6 horse statue, Green Sedayu Mall (and open-air/lesehan eateries across from it), Mall Taman Palem, Mall Daan Mogot, Plaza Cengkareng, and shophouse dining around Ruko Malibu. Local classics and quick bites feature Bakso Pak Tuk, Bakso Pendi, Seafood 88 & Seafood Bola Acui near the Citra 6 horse statue, and Warung Ibu Sri Rahayu located near GOR Cendrawasih.

Detailed Directory of Cengkareng Culinary Spots

1. Hidden Gems & Standout Favorites

  • RM Thua To: Famous for its signature Lo Mie.
  • RM Segar Wangi (Citra 8): Known for its fresh seafood dishes, located near the fish market; the Sup Kepala Salmon (salmon head soup) is a standout recommendation, and the Cumi Goreng Tepung (crispy fried squid) is a fan favorite.
  • RM Pinang Sari (Ruko Citra 2): A true hidden gem located in Citra 2 shophouses (notable for the owner's child selling stationary in front of the cashier).
  • Bakmi Air Mangkok (Near Superindo Taman Palem): A local favorite serving authentic Bangka-style noodle dishes.
  • Mie Sop Medan (Next to Fire Station / Damkar Palem): Known for serving delicious authentic Mie Sop Medan paired with Sate Kerang (clam skewers).
  • RM Ci Hera / Ci Hela: Highly rated home-style cooking recommended as a personal hidden gem.

2. Regional & Ethnic Specialties

Bakmi (Noodle Spots):

  • Bakmi A Khiong
  • Bakmi Kemanggisan
  • Bakmi Shanghai
  • Bakmi Siantar

Kwetiau Dishes:

  • Kwetiau Siram Akung (famous for family celebrations)
  • Kwetiau Goreng A Cien

Medan & Bagan Siapi-Api Specialties: Concentrated in Pasar Laris Taman Surya, offering traditional culinary items from North Sumatra and Riau.

Chinese Food: Ahua Chinese Food (near the Citra 6 horse statue).

3. Area & Street Food Hubs

Citra 8 & Lake Area: A major recreation hub around the Citra 8 lake featuring a scenic boardwalk with a long line of restaurants, cafes, and eateries offering diverse choices.

Taman Palem Hub: The main boulevard along Taman Palem is a competitive culinary strip packed with diverse regional restaurants. Recommended spots: Warung Rawit and Warung Kita.

Jalan Kayu Besar & Jalan Menceng Raya:

  • Jalan Kayu Besar: Famous for late-night comfort food, including Bebek Goreng Madura and Nasi Goreng Gila.
  • Jalan Menceng Raya: Lined with diverse food stalls, including Martabak Taraso and numerous RM Padang along the Kamal Raya-Menceng corridor.

Daan Mogot & Mall Areas:

  • Bebek Kaleyo (Daan Mogot)
  • Sate Senayan (Citra 6 area)
  • Mall Dining: Green Sedayu Mall (and the open-air/lesehan eateries right across from it), Mall Taman Palem, Mall Daan Mogot, Plaza Cengkareng, and shophouse dining around Ruko Malibu.

4. Local Classics & Quick Bites

  • Bakso Pak Tuk
  • Bakso Pendi
  • Seafood 88 & Seafood Bola Acui (near Citra 6 horse statue)
  • Warung Ibu Sri Rahayu (located near GOR Cendrawasih)
